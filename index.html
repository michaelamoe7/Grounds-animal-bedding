Why Grounds

Bedding that actually works

Great Scent

Walk into your coop and smell fresh coffee instead of ammonia. Grounds keeps odors down, so your coop smells like a café.

No Dust

Traditional bedding breaks down into dust as it decomposes. That isn't good for your flock or for you to breathe in. Grounds stays low-dust from the first scoop to the last.

Easy Clean

Droppings clump up like cat litter. Scoop out the clumps and leave the rest of the bedding in place.

How to Use

Simple as spread, scoop, repeat

  1. Spread it

    Pour Grounds across the coop floor and under the roosting bars, where your flock makes the biggest mess.

  2. Scoop it

    Droppings clump up, so you can lift them right out with a litter scoop.

  3. Top it off

    Add fresh Grounds as needed to keep the coop clean, dry and smelling good.

  4. Compost it

    When it's time to replace the bedding, add the used grounds to your compost pile, garden or yard, where they keep working as a natural soil amendment.

Our Story

From the café to the coop

Every day, tons of used coffee grounds get thrown out and sent to the landfill. We found a better use for them.

We collect used coffee grounds and turn them into an all-natural bedding that keeps coops cleaner, fresher and less dusty. It's good for your flock and good for the planet.

  • Made from recycled coffee grounds
  • 100% all natural
  • Keeps coffee waste out of landfills
